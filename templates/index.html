Comprehensive Curriculum

Academic Wings: From ECD to Class 12

Fully aligned with Nepal Government Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) and National Examination Board (NEB) guidelines.

Foundation Level

ECD & Primary (Class 1-5)

Play-based Early Childhood Development, joyful English and Nepali medium teaching, foundational numeracy, and government-supported hot nutritious Midday Meals (दिवा खाजा).

Basic Education

Basic Level (Class 6-8)

Integrated curriculum in Mathematics, Science & Technology, Social Studies, English, and Nepali, preparing students for the Class 8 District/Municipal BLE examination.

SEE Secondary

Secondary Level (Class 9-10)

Intensive preparation for Secondary Education Examination (SEE). Practical Science experiments, Optional Math, Economics, and Computer Science lab courses.

Higher Secondary (+2)

+2 Science & Management

Grade 11 & 12 streams:
• Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math
• Management: Accounting, Economics, Business Studies
• Education: Pedagogy & Social Sciences

Infrastructure & Student Life

Modern Facilities for Modern Learners

Investing in rich learning environments to ensure holistic student development.

💻

ICT & Computer Lab

Equipped with 30+ networked PCs, fiber internet, multimedia projectors, and practical coding & digital skills education from Class 4 upwards.

🔬

Science Laboratories

Separate Physics, Chemistry, and Biology workbenches with modern apparatus, reagents, and models for Class 9-12 practical demonstrations.

📚

Library & Resource Center

A diverse collection of reference textbooks, national journals, literature in Nepali & English, and competitive exam preparation resources.

🍲

Midday Meal (दिवा खाजा)

Fresh, organic, hygienically prepared nutritious daily lunches served to all primary school pupils sourced directly from local Bhadrapur farm cooperatives.

⚽

Sports & Extracurriculars

Spacious playing grounds for Volleyball, Football, Badminton, Table Tennis, along with active clubs for Junior Red Cross, Scout, and Literature.

💧

Clean Water & Sanitation

Modern RO filtered clean drinking water stations, gender-separated sanitary restrooms, and CCTV monitored secure campus premises.
